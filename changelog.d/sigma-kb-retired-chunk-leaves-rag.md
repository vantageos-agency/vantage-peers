Every RAG hit is now dropped on its own `isLatest`/`type` filterValues and on the live `memories` row (`kbMutations.latestMemoryIds`), with a 3x over-fetch. Pinned by `retiredRowsConformance.test.ts` (schema-derived classification, read-site scan, single RAG entry point), `retiredRowsNeverReturned.test.ts` (per table, org member) and `kbRetiredChunkLeavesRag.test.ts` (21 cases over recall/text/hybrid, OR-semantics fake).
