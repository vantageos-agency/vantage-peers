- **Admin CRUD B2 (messages, diary, business units, recurring tasks): a dashboard human acts in its own name on its own org's rows.** With no caller name, `messages:sendMessage` (no `from`; sender `user:<Clerk subject>`, recipients limited to the org roster, tenant from scope), `recurringTasks:create` (no `createdBy`), `recurringTasks:pause`/`resume`, `businessUnits:update` (no `callerOrchestrator`) serve a writer role (`memberWriterRoles`) of the row's own org; `messages:deleteMessage`, `diary:deleteDiary`, `businessUnits:remove` and `recurringTasks:remove` require `org:admin`. Messages and recurring tasks are keyed on their `tenantId`/`orgId` stamp (unstamped refused); diary and business units, which have no org column, on the org roster, as their readers do. One helper, `resolveHumanActor` (`convex/lib/humanActor.ts`), extended with a roster-owner compare and create mode. The cron materialises a human's schedule into the same org with `createdBy` `user:<subject>`. Agent and master paths unchanged; an org member omitting the caller on diary/message delete is now refused `role-not-writer` rather than `caller-orchestrator-required`. Evidence: convex/__tests__/commsHumanCrud.test.ts.
