- **Admin CRUD B2 (missions, briefing notes): a dashboard human acts in its own name.** With no `createdBy` / `callerOrchestrator`, `missions.create`, `missions.update`, `missions.updateStatus`, `missions.updateProgress`, `briefingNotes.create`, `briefingNotes.update` and `briefingNotes.deleteBriefingNote` now serve a member of the row's own org holding a writer role (`memberWriterRoles`), recording `user:<Clerk subject>` (`missions.lastActedBy`, new optional field; `createdBy` on create; `briefingNotes.updatedBy`). Cancelling a mission (`update` to `cancelled`, reopening a cancelled one) and `deleteBriefingNote` require `org:admin`; there is no mission delete door (cancel is the product's terminal act). A client-supplied `createdBy` is never an identity: it still has to be an orchestrator of the caller's own roster. `resolveHumanActor` gains a row-generic form (`row`, `tenantOnly`). Non-master callers of `updateStatus` / `updateProgress` now need a writer role. Agent and master paths unchanged. Evidence: convex/__tests__/missionBriefingHumanCrud.test.ts.
