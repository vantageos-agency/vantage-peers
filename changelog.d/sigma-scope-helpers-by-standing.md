- **The five scope helpers decide "fleet" on the standing `@vantageos/cloud-identity` produced, not on `scope.isMaster` (M2 part 3).** `filterByOrgScope`, `isRowVisibleToScope`, `requireScope`, `requireResolvedCaller` and `isNamespaceAllowedForScope` now share `hasFleetStanding` over `scope.standing`, which `withOrgScope` attaches once from the resolution it already performs (`fleet` for the service account, `operator-reader` for the operator org admin, `internal` for the explicit no-identity opt-in). A scope that says `isMaster: true` without that standing is no longer the fleet in any of the five. `requireResolvedCaller` and `isNamespaceAllowedForScope` keep `isMaster` as a necessary, never sufficient, conjunct only so backend-doctor 0.4.0 still reads them as refusing predicates (rule R-6). No grant is withheld from the callers that held fleet reach: the service account, the operator admin and the internal call sites keep it. A bulk-complete continuation scheduled before this release carries no standing and continues as a non-fleet scope (fail closed). Evidence: `convex/__tests__/standingById.test.ts`.
