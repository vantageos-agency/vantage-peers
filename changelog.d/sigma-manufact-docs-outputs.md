- **Every declared output schema says what each returned field means (Cloud MCP).** On the 48 tools that declare an `outputSchema`, every property at every depth, objects inside arrays included, now carries a description of what it means and when it is absent or null (322 of 322 properties, was 31 of 234). Nine schemas were aligned with what the tool returns and what its description says: `get_episode` and `get_memory` (the structured fields are under `episode`, the author is `createdBy`), `get_task`, `get_message` (`from`, `tenantId`), `get_recurring_task` (`cronExpression` and the skip fields), `get_briefing_note` (`decisions`, `linkedMemoryIds`), `get_bulk_complete_run` (`failureReason`, `orgId`), `list_broadcast_status` (`from`, `recipientInstanceId`, `readAt`), `billing_summary_by_project` (the legacy and invalid-duration counts, typed `noDurationTasks` rows), and `list_blocked_on_me` now declares each waiter's six fields with their types. Objects stay open and tool result text is unchanged. The form future schemas follow is written at the top of `mcp-server/src/outputSchemas.ts`; a registry test refuses an undescribed property and a "Returns" list that names a field the schema lacks.
