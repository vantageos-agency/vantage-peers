section: Changed
---
- **Fleet-only doors ask the package for the caller's standing; mandates act by agent ID; the publish-approval route is hardened.** `fixPatterns`, `mandates`, `issues`, `issueStatsQueries` and `errorMonitor` decide fleet standing through `convex/lib/fleetStanding.ts` (the package's `fleet` principal for the service account, the verified operator-org admin for reads only) instead of a local master flag; refusal shapes are unchanged. `mandates:accept` / `update` / `settle` require the acting agent's verified ID (`verifiedActor`) and compare it with the party's ID (`requestedById` / `fulfilledById`, stamped at `create`); the word `system` and a typed `callerOrchestrator` are no authority; budgets and costs must be finite and not negative; `update` cannot settle; `settle` takes a delivered mandate. `fixPatterns:addAttempt` refuses an acting agent outside the operator org. `POST /api/eta/verify-publish-token` requires a full 40-hex SHA and the `[ETA-APPROVED]` marker, never resolves another org's task, and no longer echoes note text, assignee or status. Roll the MCP and the backend out back to back: the four new doors (`mandates:accept` / `update` / `settle`, `fixPatterns:addAttempt`) take `verifiedActor` only on the new backend and refuse a call without it, so for agent callers they fail closed in the gap whichever side goes first; every other door is unaffected. Evidence: `convex/__tests__/fleetDoorsById.test.ts` 17/17, `convex/__tests__/audit/mandates.twoPoles.test.ts` 6/6, `convex/__tests__/audit/http.verifyPublishToken.twoPoles.test.ts` 7/7.
