- **VantagePeers Cloud: an OAuth bearer's diary reads and writes stay inside its own organisation.** A non-master bearer reaches Convex as the fleet service account, so `diary:list` read every tenant's entries whose orchestrator or author equals the bearer's userId, `diary:get` read the fleet's entry of that name, and `diary:write` stored the entry fleet-owned. `write_diary`, `get_diary` and `list_diaries` now forward the bearer's verified organisation (`verifiedOrg`, the same mechanism as `list_tasks` and the briefing-note tools), and the three Convex doors run under that organisation's member scope: its tenant range, its agent-ID roster, its stamp on write. A bearer whose organisation cannot be resolved is refused before any Convex call. The fleet master and Clerk-session members are unchanged. Additive optional argument; contract version 10 -> 11 (`BACKEND_CONTRACT_VERSION`, `REQUIRED_CONTRACT_VERSION`): roll out Convex first, MCP last. Entries an org seat wrote before this fix were stored fleet-owned and are no longer served to that seat. Task k176723g3m5cczsq44ak9fbgm58hd0tx.
