section: Security
---
- **Seat profiles and scope profiles are identified by agent ID, not by name (operator ruling 2026-10-13).** `resolveSeatAgent` no longer matches on a label: a seat profile names its agent by `oauth_scope_profiles.agentId` or names none, and a one-agent seat profile with no `agentId` refuses at mint and at refresh (`SEAT_PROFILE_UNBOUND`), never guessing a same-named agent. New operator migration `migrations/agentIdentityRows:bindSeatProfileAgentIds` (dry-run first, idempotent) binds the profiles whose single label resolved before and lists the ones it could not bind, with the reason. `fromAllowList` name lists gain `fromAllowListAgentIds` on profiles and on the tokens minted from them (expand phase: added, filled by `backfillProfileAgentIdLists` and `backfillTokenAgentIdLists`, returned by `oauth:getAccessTokenByHash` and `oauth:getScopeProfile`, copied from the PROFILE at mint, refused across organisations). `getAccessTokenByHash` states the token's and the seat agent's organisation by permanent ID and the MCP `seatAgentOf` compares those, not slugs. The writers of a profile (`upsertScopeProfile`, `patchScopeProfileEmergency`, `bindScopeProfileAgent`, `seed_client_scope_profiles`, `provisionOrganization`) keep the two lists true to each other. Runbook: `docs/cloud/runbook-seat-profile-agent-ids.md`. Evidence: `convex/__tests__/seatBoundById.test.ts`, `profileAgentIdLists.test.ts`, `scopeProfileWritersAgentIds.test.ts`, `seedClientProfilesAgentIds.test.ts`, `mcp-server/test/seat-org-by-id.test.ts`.
- **OAuth and credential hygiene (R-54, R-56).** `oauth:getScopeProfile` and `oauth:getClientByClientId` are service-account-only through `requireServiceAccount`, whose standing is the package's (`isServiceAccountScope`); `oauth:canCreateOrganization` answers from the caller's own verified session. `oauth:provisionOrganization` records who provisioned at the authorisation branch, so no audit row is ever a digest of the master secret the caller chose. `credentials:_issueToken` states its revoke-prior in the function that mints the bearer. `briefingNotes` decides fleet reach through the package's `hasFleetRead`, and its `updatedSince` bound narrows only a window already known to be complete.
