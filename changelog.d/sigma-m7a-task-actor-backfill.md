- **Task actor IDs: indexes and backfill, no door change (module M7, step 1 of 2).** New `tasks` indexes `by_assigneeId_project_status` and `by_assigneeId_status_updatedAt` (nothing reads them yet). To stay within Convex's 32-index limit per table, `by_priority` (no reader) and `by_assigneeId_status` (no reader; served by the prefix of `by_assigneeId_status_updatedAt`) are removed, so `tasks` stays at 32. An agents-row ID belongs to one org, so no org-prefixed twin is needed. `convex/__tests__/schemaIndexBudget.test.ts` now fails on any table above 32 indexes. New internal migration `migrations/backfill_task_actor_ids`: dry run by default, paginated (`cursor`, `pageSize`), prints the pre-state (rows with and without `assignedToId` / `createdById`), fills an ID only when the label resolves to exactly one agents row of the row's org, and lists ambiguous or unknown labels as `blocked` without guessing. It reuses the name-to-ID decision of `migrations/backfill_actor_ids` (three helpers exported). The switch that makes the task doors decide on these IDs ships separately, after this backfill has run in production. Evidence: `convex/__tests__/backfillTaskActorIds.test.ts` 5/5, `convex/__tests__/schemaIndexBudget.test.ts` 2/2 (red at 36 on the previous head).
