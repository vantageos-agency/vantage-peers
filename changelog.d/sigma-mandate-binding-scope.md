- **VantagePeers Cloud: `validate_mandate_spending` and `get_github_owner_bindings` no longer serve a non-master caller the whole fleet.** A non-master OAuth caller reaches Convex as the fleet service account, so `mandates:validateSpending` (gated on master only) disclosed any mandate's budget and spend to anyone holding its id, and `githubOwnerBinding:listBindings` returned every org's bindings. Both doors, and `listUnprovenMappings`, now take the transport-verified `verifiedOrg` (the mechanism `list_tasks` uses) and narrow to it: a mandate is the operator org's, so a client org is refused `RBAC_DENIED` before any read; bindings and unproven mappings are filtered to the caller's org. The master path is unchanged. Backend contract version 12. Evidence: `convex/__tests__/mandateBindingVerifiedOrg.test.ts`, `mcp-server/test/mandate-binding-doors-verified-org.test.ts` (task k17e1twdq22k9311yygkfgx9d58hcsws).
