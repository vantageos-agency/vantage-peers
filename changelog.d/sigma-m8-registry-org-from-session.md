- **The agent registry takes its organisation from the verified session, never from an argument (Cloud, module M8, task k17dcydcvcdzh1gp1zd1ebt23s8fyjde).** `agents:registerAgent|setAgentAddress|deactivateAgent|reactivateAgent|renameAgent|getAgent|listAgentsByOrg`, `agentCredentials:mintAgentCredential|revokeAgentCredential|getAgentCredentialStatus` and `agentRelations:linkChild|unlinkChild|childrenOf|parentsOf|graphByOrg` no longer accept `orgSlug`: a call that still sends it is rejected by the validator, and the organisation a door acts in is the one the session's `org_id` resolves to through `@vantageos/cloud-identity` (`resolveCallerFromClaims` + `assertOrgAdmin`, one adapter `resolveAdminSession` in `convex/lib/agentIdentity.ts`). The target agent is admitted by comparing its STORED org ID with the principal's (`assertTargetBelongsTo`); an `agents` row carrying no `clerkOrgId` is refused `target-unstamped` instead of being matched by its slug. `registerAgent` and `mintAgentCredential` stamp the verified org's id and current label; the local `requireOrgAdminById`, `stampOrgRefusal` and the `withOrgScope` stamp comparison are deleted, `anonymousRefusal` now relays the package's own `no-verified-subject` refusal. BREAKING for callers: the dashboard's `components/agents/*` must stop sending `orgSlug` before or in step with this deploy; `scripts/mint-station-agents.mjs`, `scripts/prove-station-agent-credential.mjs`, `scripts/provision-client-org-agents.mjs` and the runbook read-back are updated here. PRECONDITION: every `agents` row must carry `clerkOrgId` (`migrations/backfill_org_clerk_id` for `agents` run on prod) or the doors refuse it. Still open and named, not closed: `findAgentByName` (kept `@deprecated` for `auth.ts`/`messages.ts`/`inboxReader.ts`/`actorIds.ts`/`seatAgent.ts`/`fleetActors.ts`) and `assertAgentNameFree`. `convex/__tests__/agentIdentityById.test.ts` section 5, `convex/__tests__/agentRegistryNoNameSelection.test.ts`.
