- **A source whose Client ID Metadata Document fetch timed out can no longer keep the /authorize or /token pool full.** About 6 source addresses hanging distinct URLs on 2 domains could hold all 3 slots (10 fetches a minute each, 3 s timeout). A source (IPv4, or IPv6 /64) whose /authorize or /token fetch times out is now suspect for 60 s, and all suspect sources together may hold at most 1 slot of a pool (`CIMD_SUSPECT_LANE`), so at least 2 of 3 stay for sources that have not hung; a refresh is never affected. Chosen over the sentinel's domain-level cooldown after 3 distinct sources (an attacker with many domains and 3 addresses per domain still fills the pool), over a slot reserved for cached documents (a first-time client has no cached document) and over capping a domain at 1 fetch (the attacker uses more domains): the lane keys on the SOURCE, so a stranger can only demote itself and never cools a victim down (pass-1 F1). Residual, stated: an attacker with many fresh addresses gets one timeout per address and minute (about 40 addresses to hold 2 of 3 slots), and three fresh sources hanging at the very same instant block a fresh victim for one timeout (3 s). Per-process, like the other limits. No Convex function, argument or return changes: backend contract stays 27. Tests: `mcp-server/test/cimd-pool-lane.test.ts`. Task k171z3pbydgfb6rp3tpns3a4k18hynws.
