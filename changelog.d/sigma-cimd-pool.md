section: Changed
---
- **Hanging Client ID Metadata Documents can no longer keep the /authorize or /token pool full from one network.** Before: 3 slots per path, so about 6 addresses hanging distinct URLs held them all. Now: (1) the /authorize and /token pools are 8 slots each (a fetch is at most 5 KB and 3 s, so 8 + 8 + 2 refresh is at most 90 KB in flight); refresh keeps its 2; (2) one source PREFIX (IPv4 /24, IPv6 /48) holds at most 3 slots of a path, so rotating addresses inside a prefix cannot fill a pool; (3) a prefix that timed out twice within 60 s is suspect for 60 s, and all suspect prefixes together hold at most 1 slot of a path. One timeout marks nothing (a shared address, NAT or egress with one slow document is not squeezed), the `unknown` source (no address header) is never marked and never prefix-capped (it keeps the per-source bound of 2), and a refresh is never held back. The key is the source prefix, never a domain or a URL, so a stranger can only demote its own prefix and cannot cool a victim down (pass-1 F1). **Attack cost.** A prefix holds 3 slots, so blocking a fresh victim takes 3 prefixes hanging at the same instant (a 3 s outage), and keeping 7 non-lane slots filled takes 3 fresh prefixes per 3 s timeout, because a prefix is lane-limited for 60 s after its second timeout: about 60 distinct /24 (or /48) a minute, plus the one lane slot; the previous design fell to about 6 addresses, then to about 40 reused addresses of one /24. Residual, stated: an attacker with that many prefixes still wins (an IPv6 /32 holds 65,536 /48); the limits are per process, so they multiply by the replica count. Chosen over the sentinel's domain-level cooldown after 3 distinct sources (an attacker with many domains still fills the pool), over a slot reserved for cached documents (a first-time client has none) and over capping a domain at 1 fetch (the attacker uses more domains). No Convex function, argument or return changes: backend contract stays 27. Tests: `mcp-server/test/cimd-pool-lane.test.ts`. Task k171z3pbydgfb6rp3tpns3a4k18hynws.
