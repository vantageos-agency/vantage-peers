- **The stats, improvisation-digest and issue-stats doors refuse the fleet service account that carries no verified agent, and narrow before they bound.** `stats:orchestratorStats`, `stats:openTaskCountsByOrchestrator`, `stats:fleetStats`, `improvisationDigest:scanWindow` and `issueStatsQueries:getLatest` took the claimless service account as master over every tenant. Each now declares `verifiedActor` and, for the service account, requires it (`requireFleetAgentActor`): no claim is refused with `RBAC_DENIED verified-actor-required`, an agent of another organisation with `ORG_MISMATCH`, and a non-service-account caller presenting the claim with `verified-actor-not-trusted`. `issueStatsQueries:getLatest` also raises for an ordinary org member instead of answering `[]`. `orchestratorStats` streams the member's org and applies the roster per row before the 5000 cap, and `scanWindow` reads the tenant and time window as index ranges in bounded pages, so an out-of-roster or foreign flood can no longer push a visible row out of the window. MCP: `improvisationDigest:scanWindow` joins `VERIFIED_ACTOR_DOORS`; contract version 3 to 4 (Convex first, MCP last); an old MCP that sends no claim gets the refusal, not a crash. Tests: `convex/__tests__/unownedModules.twoPoles.test.ts` 11/11.
