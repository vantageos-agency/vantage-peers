- **VantagePeers Cloud and Self-host: a repo no station owns is recorded on its mapping row.** `add_repo_mapping` takes `outOfScope: { reason }` (trimmed, at least 10 characters) instead of an orchestrator: a row is either routed or out of scope, and `outOfScope` beside an orchestrator, a reviewer or an agent ID is refused (`INVALID_ROUTE`); re-saving an out-of-scope row with an orchestrator routes it again, and the conversion leaves no half of the old route behind. `get_repo_route` answers `{ state: "out-of-scope", reason }` for your own organisation's row only, and `check_repo_routing` returns the repos of your list recorded this way in a new `outOfScope` bucket, not in `missing`, so they do not block `complete`. In an organisation that adopted explicit review routing, a task or review task naming an out-of-scope repo is refused with `RBAC_DENIED` reason `repo-out-of-scope` (the text gives the reason and says to route it with `add_repo_mapping` and an owner); an organisation that has not adopted routing is unchanged. The webhook ignores events of an out-of-scope repo. `githubRepoMapping.orchestrator` becomes optional (additive: every stored row keeps it).
