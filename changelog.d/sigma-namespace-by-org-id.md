section: Changed
---
- **Team namespaces are keyed by the organisation's Clerk org ID (`team/<clerkOrgId>`), never by its slug.** A slug rename no longer strands an org's namespace and a freed slug taken by a new org no longer reaches the old org's rows. `isNamespaceAllowedForScope` decides with `sameOrg` from `@vantageos/cloud-identity` on `orgClerkId` (`convex/lib/auth.ts`, `convex/lib/teamNamespace.ts` for the shape only); `search`, `memoriesScoped` and the KB actions share it, and `resolveOrgScopeForAction` returns `orgClerkId`. New internal migration `migrations/namespace_by_org_id` (`inventory`, `run` dry-run by default, `readBack`) rewrites `memories.namespace`, the `oauth_access_tokens` / `oauth_scope_profiles` prefixes, and `kbUploads.orgId` / `uploadTickets.orgId`; an unmapped slug is refused and listed, never guessed. The upload flow (`generateUploadUrl`, `generateUploadUrlWithTicket`, `claimUpload`, the OKF export binding) is keyed by the org ID too, so generate, claim and `kb:storeDocumentChunked` accept the same `orgId`; before this, an org member's upload was refused at ingest. **Deploy order (no slug fallback):** between the deploy and a clean `readBack`, `team/<slug>` rows, upload bindings and tickets are withheld from their own org (empty results, no leak). Deploy, then `inventory`, then `run` with `dryRun: false` (every table, paged to `isDone`), then `readBack` until `clean: true`, then one scoped-identity read of a migrated row, all before traffic. Ships after M2 part 3. `tenantNamespaceBounds(orgClerkId)` (the index-bounds form of the same rule, shape from #1511) derives from `teamNamespaceOf`; `convex/__tests__/tenantNamespaceBoundsEquivalence.test.ts` pins that it covers exactly what `isNamespaceAllowedForScope` admits, for a renamed org and a reused slug. When #1511 lands first, rebase and pass `scope.orgClerkId` at its `episodes.ts` call.
